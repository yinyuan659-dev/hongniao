Privacy Policy隐私政策

Yixianqian (the "Platform") takes your privacy seriously. This Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information. 1. Information we collect (1) Registration information: nickname, avatar, age and gender; (2) Matchmaking profile: height, education, occupation, income, marital history, interests, partner preferences and similar information you provide in the questionnaire and the AI interview, and the profile generated from it; (3) Device information: a device identifier, used to bind your account to your device, manage signed-in devices and recover your account; (4) Location: with your permission, used for same-city recommendations and to show distance; (5) Identity verification: the name, ID number and face scan you provide are checked by the verification provider. We keep only the result (passed or not) and an irreversible digest of the ID number (used for the one-person-one-account rule and account recovery). We do not keep the ID number itself or any face image; (6) Usage records: chat messages, moments, call records, likes and matches, and AI feature usage, and your Qiandou top-up and spending records; (7) Camera, microphone and photo library: used only when you do a face scan, make an audio or video call, or send photos or voice messages. 2. How we use information (1) To provide core features: matchmaking profile, match recommendations, messaging, audio and video calls, and moments; (2) To show your matchmaking profile to users of the opposite sex that the Platform recommends to you; (3) Same-city recommendations and distance calculation; (4) Account security, identity verification and handling of violations; (5) Settlement and reconciliation of Qiandou (fees for video calls, AI features and identity verification). 3. Third-party services To provide the relevant features, we give the necessary information to the following providers: (1) Identity verification: Alibaba Cloud (ID Verification), which processes your name, ID number and face scan; (2) Artificial intelligence: DeepSeek, which processes what you type in the AI interview and the profile information used to generate your profile summary, recommendation reasons and conversation starters. Apart from this, we do not sell your personal information to any third party. We provide it only with your explicit consent, or when required by laws and regulations or lawfully requested by regulators. 4. Storage and protection (1) Chat messages and AI interview content are stored encrypted; (2) Your own model API key is stored encrypted, and only the last four characters are shown; (3) Data is stored on servers located in mainland China. 5. Your rights (1) You can change your nickname, avatar and matchmaking profile at any time (except items recorded as "things to note" in the AI interview); (2) You can delete moments you posted and clear your chat history; (3) You can view and remove the devices that can sign in to your account under "Signed-in devices"; (4) To delete your account, contact Platform support. 6. Protection of minors The Platform does not provide services to users under 18. If we find that a minor is using the Platform, we will immediately delete the account and its information. 7. Updates to this Policy This Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on the Platform.
